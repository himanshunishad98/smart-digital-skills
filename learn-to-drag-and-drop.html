Learn to Drag & Drop

PUZZLE LAB · PRE‑READER EDITION

What this activity builds

A gentle first stop before real coding puzzles: kids practice clicking, holding, dragging, and releasing so the mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen stops being the hard part later on.

  • Model it first — drag a piece yourself, including an "oops" drop, so mistakes look normal.
  • Say the steps out loud: point, press and hold, move, let go — that's a sequence!
  • Pair students up and let the first partner drive while the second cheers them on.
  • Let learners retry as often as they like — there's no penalty for a missed drop.
